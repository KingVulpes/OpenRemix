Original sampler: Eric Heitz, Laurent Belcour, Victor Ostromoukhov,
David Coeurjolly and Jean-Claude Iehl, "A Low-Discrepancy Sampler that
Distributes Monte Carlo Errors as a Blue Noise in Screen Space", 2019.
